ClearVoice. Speak naturally. Be understood clearly. She holds one button, and the microphone is open only while she holds it. No background listening, ever. She speaks the way she can: I need, appointment, Tuesday, move. ClearVoice writes down what it heard, and offers one clear sentence: could you move the appointment to Tuesday, please. Nothing is spoken yet. The sentence is on the screen, big enough to read, and she can change it before it is said. She edits one word. She taps Speak it. The voice runs on this laptop, in about a second, with no connection and no upload. Her names, her street, her treatment, go into a small private dictionary, and it hears her better each week. When it misses, it says so plainly, and she starts again. That is the whole device: hold, speak, check, speak. Audio processed this session only. Nothing saved. ClearVoice may help people be understood more clearly. It is not a medical device, and it does not replace speech therapy.
